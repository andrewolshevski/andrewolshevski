I'm Head of Product & Learning at Bedford, a school that helps professionals grow their brand and business online. I build the curriculum and the technology behind it.

I've spent over a decade in education, most of it building products that help people actually learn (and stick with it). I started as a French teacher at Fluent City, where I eventually became Head of Product and led the company to acquisition. My background as a teacher still drives how I build. 3 quick principles:

  • Do → Reflect > Study → Do
  • Computers : teachers :: Humans : calculators
